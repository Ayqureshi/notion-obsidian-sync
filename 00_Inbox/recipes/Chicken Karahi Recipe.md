
### Ingredients (Optimized)

- **1 lb** bone-in chicken (cut into small pieces)
    
- **1** small onion, chopped
    
- **1 tbsp** garlic, minced or crushed _(increased)_
    
- **1.5 tsp** ginger, minced or crushed _(increased)_
    
- **2** tomatoes, chopped (peel the skins first if you have a spare minute!)
    
- **1 tbsp** Greek yogurt _(new)_
    
- **Spices:** $\frac{3}{4}$ tsp salt, $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp red pepper, $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp turmeric _(new)_, $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp ground cumin _(new)_, $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp ground coriander _(new)_
    
- **Finishing Spice:** $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp garam masala _(new)_
    
- **Garnish:** 1 green chili (sliced), julienned ginger, cilantro


- **Sauté the Aromatics & Chicken:**
    
    Heat your oil in the pan over **medium-high heat**. Add the chicken, chopped onion, the increased garlic (1 tbsp), and ginger (1.5 tsp). Sauté uncovered, stirring frequently, until the onions are translucent and the chicken is lightly sealed and has changed color.
    
- **Add Tomatoes & Ground Spices:**
    
    Add your chopped tomatoes, salt, red pepper, and your new spices: **turmeric, ground cumin, and ground coriander**.
    
- **Cook Uncovered:**
    
    Keeping the heat at **medium-high**, continue cooking uncovered. Stir regularly, using your spoon to help mash down the tomatoes as they soften. Cook until the tomato moisture has mostly evaporated, the chicken is fully cooked through, and you start to see the oil separating and glistening at the edges of the thick masala.
    
- **Swirl in the Yogurt & Garam Masala:**
    
    Turn the heat down to low for just a moment (to prevent splitting), and stir in **1 tbsp of Greek yogurt** and **$\frac{1}{2}$ tsp of garam masala**. Stir rapidly to incorporate it into a smooth, glossy sauce, then bring the heat back up for 1–2 minutes to let it bubble and thicken.
    
- **Garnish and Serve:**
    
    Turn off the heat. Scatter your sliced green chili, julienned ginger, and fresh cilantro over the top. Serve hot!
