
### Ingredients 

- 1 kg **bone-in turkey** (breast, thigh, or leg)
    
- 1 large **leek**, sliced into rounds
    
- 2-3 stalks **celery**, sliced on a bias
    
- 3-4 packed cups **fresh baby spinach** (scaled up slightly since it wilts down)
    
- 2 tbsp avocado oil
    
- 2 tbsp **curry powder**
    
- 2 shallots, chopped
    
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
    
- 2” piece of ginger, minced
    
- 2 (14 oz) cans full-fat coconut milk
    
- 1 cup water
    
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce (slightly adjusted for volume)
    
- 1.5 tbsp coconut sugar
    
- 1/2 cup fresh **Thai basil leaves**
    
- 1 **lime**, zest and juice
    
- A large pot of steamed jasmine white rice


**1.Brown the turkey:**10 min.

Heat 1 tbsp of avocado oil in your largest deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Season the whole bone-in turkey piece generously with salt and white pepper. Sear it for 4–5 minutes per side until the skin/surface is deeply golden brown. Remove the turkey and set it on a plate (it won't be cooked through yet).

**2.Soften the aromatics & bloom:**6-7 min.

Turn the heat down to medium. Add the other tbsp of oil if the pan looks dry. Toss in the celery, leeks, and shallots with a pinch of salt. Sauté for 4 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute. Sprinkle the curry powder over the vegetables and stir constantly for 60 seconds to bloom the spices in the hot fat.

**3.The slow coconut braise:**35-45 min.

Pour in both cans of full-fat coconut milk, the soy sauce, coconut sugar, and 1 cup of water. Stir well. Place the browned turkey piece (and any juices) right into the liquid. Bring to a boil, then immediately drop the heat to low. Cover the pan with a lid and let it simmer softly for 35–45 minutes, turning the turkey over halfway through, until the meat is completely tender.

**4.Shred the meat:**5 min.

Lift the turkey out onto a clean cutting board. Use two forks to easily pull the meat off the bone and shred it into bite-sized pieces. Discard the bones and skin.

**5.Reunite and taste:**2 min.

Slide the shredded turkey back into the bubbling curry sauce. Give it a good stir and let it warm through for 2 minutes. Taste the sauce right now—add a bit more kosher salt if you need to pop the flavors.

**6.The fresh green finish:**1 min, off heat.

Turn off the heat. Fold in the fresh baby spinach, Thai basil leaves, and the lime zest and juice. The spinach will wilt in about 60 seconds into the rich broth.
